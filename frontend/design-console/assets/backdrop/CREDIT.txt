Mission page backdrop
=====================

File            orion-hero.webp
Source          orion-source.jpeg (1408x768)
Origin          Generated artwork, supplied by Ollie. Not an observation, not a
                photograph, and not derived from survey data.

Because it is generated rather than captured, there is no third-party copyright
to clear and no attribution owed. What it does need is honesty: the Mission page
labels it "generated artwork - not an observation" rather than captioning it as
a real frame of M 42. Every other number on that page is measured, and a
synthetic sky presented as a real one would be the one fabricated thing on a
console built to avoid exactly that.

The previous backdrop was ESO's HAWK-I view of M 42 (eso1625a, CC BY 4.0,
credit ESO/H. Drass et al.). It was replaced at Ollie's request. If a real frame
is ever wanted here again, that one is a drop-in: it needs the ESO credit line
restored to .hero-credit, because CC BY 4.0 requires the credit stay visible.

Adaptations made
    Cropped to 16:9 about the centre (the source is slightly wider than 16:9,
    so the trim is horizontal).
    Brightness x1.15, saturation x0.90.
    Re-encoded to WebP, quality 94.
Reproduce with  python3 tools/backdrop.py

The source is kept beside the derived file so the transform can be redone or
revised without needing the original again.
